about

doors to hallways is a cheeky collection of riddles, mirrors, and doorways that never really lead anywhere, or perhaps perpetually lead somewhere else.

i hope it feels like wandering through a big house at night looking for the bathroom, which you're pretty sure is just around the corner, but instead, as you keep opening doors, you only find more doors, long hallways, and dead ends.

each scene is its own experiment in the materiality of websites and interfaces with a slight twist.
